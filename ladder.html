Real NAY = ENAY − (YTM − Real yield): BlackRock’s nominal TIPS YTM embeds the market’s inflation estimate; subtracting the YTM−real-yield gap strips it out. If a real yield is blank, Real NAY falls back to ENAY − inflation assumption (marked †). Muni ENAY is BlackRock’s plain (tax-exempt) ENAY; no tax-equivalent adjustment is made.
